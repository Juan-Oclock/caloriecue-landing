## Nutrition audit

Downloaded the official USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy April 2018 JSON archive on November 2, 2026:

`https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_json_2018-04.zip`

These are historical reference averages, not new food measurements. All 13 foods use records from the same dataset. The draft links each record and the official dataset download page. FDA label guidance supports the explanation of 2,000 calories as a general reference rather than a personal requirement.

Lunch: 120g cooked roasted skinless chicken breast (171477), 180g cooked white long-grain rice (168878), 150g boiled drained broccoli (169967), 10g olive oil (171413).

Snack: 180g edible apple with skin (171688), 25g plain almonds (170567).

Dinner: 150g cooked farmed Atlantic salmon (175168), 250g baked potato with skin (170093), 150g boiled drained carrots (170394), 10g olive oil (171413).

The calculation uses Decimal arithmetic and rounds only for display. Rounded ingredient rows can differ from a meal total by 1–2 kcal; this is explained in the article. Both 10g oil portions are included. Water is the assumed drink. Oil, sauces and different preparation methods must be adjusted for a reader's actual meal.

Reproduce the audit with:

```sh
python3 output/blog-research/2000-calories/calculate.py /path/to/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_json_2018-04.zip
```

The selected per-100g reference values, food weights, and calculated nutrients are saved in `output/blog-research/2000-calories/nutrition-audit.json`.
